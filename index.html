Load a photo and press Play: a band of light slowly sweeps across it, and the colors inside the band ring out as sound. The composition of the photo becomes the score. Touch or trace the photo to layer sounds on top.

Play by touching the photo

Touch or trace the photo to add a processed accent of the color under your finger, layered softly over Scan. It works with Scan stopped too, and with several fingers at once.

GLITCH

Reads the photo as raw data and breaks it. Rows of pixels are played directly as waveforms, and the colors detune each other in a feedback loop. The read head jumps around the photo at random, stutters, runs backwards and suddenly falls silent.

  • Scan: every color plays continuously, and colors inside the moving band are louder. The composition of the photo is the score
  • The strongest color (area × saturation) sets an emotion, which picks the scale and key for the whole photo
  • Hue → position in that scale (over 2 octaves). Gray tones use brightness instead
  • Brightness → volume (very dark colors drop an octave) / Saturation → timbre (soft ↔ sharp)
  • Area → how much of each color lingers during Scan / Emotion → a sonic character for each color
  • Glitch: raw R, G, B pixel values become waveforms, and more saturated colors disturb the others more strongly

The Color Sound series

Version 10 reads a single photo as a score. Its sibling instruments play whatever your camera sees, live:

  • Gazer — every cell's color becomes a roaring, distorted wall of sound
  • Hazer — colors drift into slow ambient sound, with optional Satie-style piano
  • Scanner — a scan bar sweeps across the screen and plays the colors it passes
